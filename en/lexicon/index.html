WORDS MEAN THINGS

The AGD lexicon.

Words for thinking about distribution. These concepts belong to the framework proposed by TokPortal; they are not presented as academic consensus.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Account Isolation

Account Isolation is the AGD principle that gives every account a sufficiently distinct identity, audience and purpose to operate as an independent editorial surface.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Account Persona

An Account Persona is the recurring identity that gives an account a perspective: who speaks, to whom, and why people return.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Account-Generated Distribution

Account-Generated Distribution (AGD) organizes differentiated social accounts as organic distribution infrastructure. More accounts only make strategic sense when audiences, voices and creative work also differ.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Agentic AGD

TokPortal MCP exposes social operations to an agent. A preview uses top-level dry_run: true, with creation parameters inside body; the decision to execute live remains a separate step.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

AI AGD

AI AGD means using AI to plan, produce, analyze or orchestrate AGD. It does not imply fully autonomous execution.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Content Isolation

Content Isolation preserves meaningful creative differences between accounts: narrative, angle, format or purpose. It complements Account Isolation, which differentiates distribution surfaces.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Geo-Posting

Geo-Posting means publishing through an account operated in the target market. Content must still fit that audience’s uses, references and constraints.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Geo-Targeted Account

A Geo-Targeted Account is established and operated in its target market. Geography is one execution context among others, without guaranteeing audience composition.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Multi-Account Strategy

A Multi-Account Strategy distributes a brand’s experiments across differentiated accounts. It is AGD’s organizational foundation, not its complete definition.

In the AGD lexicon, a Native Account is created and maintained in the real consumer application environment. The definition describes execution.

Native Warming

Native Warming happens in the real application through ordinary, authentic interactions. The practice should remain meaningful to the person performing it.

Niche Warming

Niche Warming means exploring niche content in the app through searches, viewing and authentic interactions. Its precise effect on reach remains a testable hypothesis.

pSEO AGD

pSEO AGD systematically organizes social content around distinct search intents. Volume does not excuse redundant pages or posts.

SEO AGD

SEO AGD explores social content and properties appearing in search engines and platform search. This possibility guarantees neither indexing nor ranking.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Slideshow AGD

Slideshow AGD uses accounts publishing native carousels or slideshows. A useful sequence progresses, giving a clear reason to reach the next screen.

AGD / CANONICAL CONCEPT

Viral Hit

A Viral Hit is an unusually high distribution outcome relative to a defined baseline: account, cohort or campaign. A fixed view threshold does not fit every context.
