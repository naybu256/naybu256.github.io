Brand Memes on TikTok: What Ryanair’s Persona Can Teach You

1 / 3 · YOUR OWN PACE

Useful material often comes from a friction your audience instantly recognizes. Three meetings to decide to cancel one, for example. Show the text to someone in the intended audience. Ask what they recognized, not whether they liked it. Qualitative feedback helps separate resonance from politeness.

The punchline can target a habit rather than a person. A recognizable shared behavior provides material without inventing customer quotes. Keep a fictional situation’s status clear. Comic precision often comes from one convincing detail rather than a spectacular claim about how many people experience the scene. That leaves space for humor while preserving the distinction between an observation, an illustration and actual research.

Keep the cultural reference attached to the audience problem.

A meme works through shared recognition. The audience supplies part of the meaning, which can make a small image or sentence feel unusually economical. That economy disappears when a brand uses a format without understanding the situation people recognise inside it. Start by describing the joke without naming the trend. If nobody on the team can explain the tension, borrowing its appearance is unlikely to produce a coherent adaptation.

Try the post without the logo and without the CTA. Does the joke still land for the intended audience? Then restore the product and ask whether it adds a relevant detail or merely interrupts the punchline. Some entertaining posts can support a broader account identity without selling anything directly, but that should be an editorial choice rather than an excuse attached after the fact. Keep rights and source context in the production record. A reference can be culturally familiar while its underlying image, music or clip still requires careful handling in a commercial workflow.

The Ryanair reference is a video, and that distinction matters. Use it to examine personification and brand context, not as evidence that a slideshow version would perform the same way. To adapt the mechanism into original slides, make the first frame establish the character, the second introduce the specific tension and the last deliver a payoff that depends on the brand's situation. If the joke still works identically after replacing the airline with a shampoo bottle, it may be a generic meme with a logo attached. Keep the number of frames dictated by the joke; stretching a three-beat idea into ten cards is a remarkably efficient way to kill it.
