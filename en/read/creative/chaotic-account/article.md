## The argument

A founder can post a screenshot, an anecdote and a demo without losing their voice. The connection is their view of work. Organize content by purpose: explain, entertain, expose friction. Otherwise the week’s strongest post becomes an excuse to chase every trend.

Imagine a week built around missed appointments: a meme, an anecdote and a practical demonstration. Formats change while the problem remains recognizable. Compare responses within that content family. You can learn which treatment clarifies the situation without reducing the entire editorial direction to the format producing the biggest spike. This preserves flexibility while providing a meaningful basis for the next creative decision.

## Change the surface without losing the narrator.

A chaotic account can move from a joke to a demonstration to an unexpected reply while remaining recognisable. The continuity may live in its obsessions, timing or point of view rather than its visual template. That is different from publishing whatever the team happens to have ready. An editorial identity gives viewers a reason to accept a surprising post because it still feels like something this account would notice.

Write a small set of behavioural rules instead of a rigid layout guide. Define the kinds of tension the account enjoys, the subjects it understands and the claims it will not make. Review a mixed batch together and ask which post breaks the identity. Keep the discussion separate from whether each post is individually funny. When testing chaos against a recurring series, compare similar audiences and useful promises, not a carefully produced experiment against leftovers. A flexible account still needs records of its concepts, otherwise nobody can explain what the apparent randomness is teaching the team.

A chaotic account still needs a writer's room, even if that room is one person and a notes app. Keep a short list of recurring tensions, references the audience understands and lines the brand will not cross. The apparent disorder can live in the timing, the escalation or the visual treatment. The underlying promise should remain legible. Without that core, the account becomes a random collection of internet gestures, and every new post has to earn recognition from zero. The Duo example below offers a way to discuss serial fiction; it is not permission to copy the character or claim that shock alone caused the campaign's response.

### Coherence beneath changing formats

- **Point of view:** A recognisable way of noticing the world.

- **Boundaries:** Subjects and claims that belong to this identity.

- **Surprise:** A new execution that still feels editorially plausible.
