## The takeaway

In the AGD lexicon, a Native Account is created and maintained in the real consumer application environment. The definition describes execution.

## The argument

Native operation can provide access to the flows and features ordinary creators use. It also introduces timing, permissions and human tasks to organize. Nothing in this definition guarantees an audience. A good brief separates execution requirements from distribution outcomes measured afterward.

In a brief, replace “quality account” with checkable conditions: working access, a profile matching the plan and required features available. Add who checks them and when. This precision helps resolve incidents without debating an adjective. Creative quality and audience outcomes can then be judged from their own observations. Keeping these questions separate makes both procurement and editorial analysis easier to understand.

## A credible account is an accumulation of coherent choices.

An account can be created in the right environment and still feel interchangeable. The name, biography, recent posts and replies need to describe the same useful person or editorial project. Imagine following a cooking account after a clear weeknight recipe, then finding its next posts are unrelated motivation and generic app promotions. The problem is not a missing technical ritual. The account has failed to maintain the expectation that earned the follow.

Document the visible promise alongside the operating details. Which situations does the account cover? Which language and references belong there? What kinds of comments deserve a useful response? Keep access and execution records accurate, but do not treat an account’s age, device or location as an explanation for its audience relationship. Those variables can matter to operations without proving a distribution advantage. When performance changes, inspect publication status and creative fit separately. A technical account record and an editorial account brief answer different questions; a mature workflow keeps both.

### Two records, one coherent account

- **Identity:** The audience promise visible in the profile and posts.

- **Operations:** Current access, status and execution context.

- **Continuity:** The expectations the next post should preserve.
