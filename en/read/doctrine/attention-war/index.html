THE TAKEAWAY

For an app, a useful AGD creative connects a recognisable audience problem to a visible product action. In this StudyFetch post, the creator introduces a specific memorisation task, shows the corresponding prompt on a laptop and exposes the generated answer. The connection is the lesson to study.

    The creator speaks to camera about memorising four terms and counts them on her fingers.

    Our read. The opening lets a relevant viewer recognise their own problem. It gives the later app appearance a reason to be in the video, beyond a generic claim that AI saves time.

    Show the request

    The view changes to a filmed laptop with StudyFetch’s chat and a prompt about the same memorisation task.

    Our read. The continuity matters: the product addresses the exact problem introduced earlier. A cut to an unrelated dashboard would supply branding while withholding the useful connection.

    Expose the output

    The answer contains a mnemonic with words mapped to the terms; the clip then returns to the creator.

    Our read. The viewer can inspect what the tool produced. The output’s presence is observable; its accuracy and usefulness still require subject-matter checking. That distinction should survive any adaptation.

THE SIGNAL

Give the app a job in the story.

1 / 3 · YOUR OWN PACE

A stranger scrolling past your app does not owe it a feature tour. Give them a problem worth staying for. The StudyFetch clip above does that with a narrow study task, then keeps the same task in view when the product appears. The app earns its place in the scene. You can examine that continuity without pretending to know what the post generated in installs or revenue.

This is a more useful reference for an app founder than an instruction to “be authentic”. The creator, question and product action fit together on screen. A different account could address a different kind of student or a different moment in their work, but the same edit cannot prove that a whole account network would perform. The unit we can inspect here is one published creative.

Keep the same question from hook to answer.

The first editorial decision is the audience’s job. In this case it is a specific memorisation problem, not “students who like technology”. That level of detail is what makes an account brief useful. A student preparing a presentation, someone revising a terminology list and someone organising lecture notes might all use the same product, but they would need different demonstrations. Write the task, the moment and the expected output before you write an opening line. Then check whether the actual product can deliver the output you are planning to show. If it cannot, change the concept instead of asking the edit to conceal the gap.

The camera’s move to the laptop is the hinge of the creative. It connects the recommendation to something a viewer can inspect: a question and its answer. The oblique screen recording is not pristine studio footage, and that should not become a new rule that roughness performs better. What matters for this reading is that the operation stays connected to the opening. In your own version, favour legibility. Crop out irrelevant browser chrome, make the input readable and leave enough time for a viewer to understand the output. A fast cut can create pace while still leaving the useful part impossible to examine.

For an AGD team, account differentiation should start before the recording. Give each account a reason to ask a different question of the product. One could centre on planning a revision session, another on checking understanding after a lecture, and another on making a group presentation. These are proposed editorial directions, not accounts we observed in the StudyFetch campaign. Each needs its own visible task and an honest voice. Repeating one script through several profiles gives you more publication instances; it does not automatically give you more audience insight.

Keep a creative record that survives the upload. Save the source of inspiration, your own recording, the concept ID, the changed variable and the final post URL. If a new opening performs differently, you need to know whether the demonstration also changed. Read comments for the job people are asking about, then compare that with the destination you offer. A viewer asking whether the app handles lecture recordings should not land on a page that says nothing about recordings. UTMs can help trace a visit; the purchase still needs its own event record. Treat a public view count as a reason to inspect a creative, not as a substitute for this chain.

At handoff, the operator should receive a finished package: original media with the necessary rights, a caption that matches the demonstrated task, an account identifier and a valid publishing window. TokPortal’s video-slot workflow below makes those inputs explicit. The product helps move an approved idea into an operational queue. The part it cannot decide for you is whether the original promise survives the upload. Watch the final asset once as somebody who has never heard of your app. If the product appears without answering the opening question, return to the brief.

Prepare the actual video slot, not just the caption

TokPortal’s editor distinguishes Video, Carousel and Story, and asks for a target publishing period. The API follows the same operational idea: the content needs a valid format, accessible media and a usable window.

MCP REQUEST
{
  "tool": "tokportal_configure_bundle_video",
  "arguments": {
    "id": "<real-bundle-uuid>",
    "position": 1,
    "body": {
      "video_type": "video",
      "video_url": "<public_url returned by your video upload>",
      "description": "A practical example of organising freelance subscriptions.",
      "target_publish_date": "<valid future date: YYYY-MM-DD>"

Replace the marked values with your actual upload and bundle. This template is intentionally not a runnable campaign. target_publish_date starts a two-day publishing window; it does not request an exact minute.

  1. 01
    Choose the correct media fields

    A video needs video_url and description. A TikTok carousel instead needs carousel_images and tiktok_sound_url.

  2. 02
    Use a valid window

    Allow at least three days while account creation is pending, or one day for delivered/existing accounts. Check the current response and limits.

  3. 03
    Inspect readiness

    GET /api/ext/bundles/{id}/publish-readiness lists blockers without a debit or mutation. Review ignored_fields and the returned start/end dates.
