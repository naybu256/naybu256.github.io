1 / 3 · YOUR OWN PACE

Turn one question into two suitable treatments without forcing video into an animated slideshow. Choose a useful shared metric, such as measurable qualified visits. Keep format-native metrics separate. The test compares content experiences, not file extensions.

Include production cost in the table. A video may achieve a stronger raw outcome with much more work, while a slideshow may make an explanation easier to revise. The choice depends on the editorial question and resources. Present the tradeoff rather than crowning a format for every situation. Also retain whether both treatments actually delivered equivalent information; unequal substance can otherwise masquerade as a format effect.
