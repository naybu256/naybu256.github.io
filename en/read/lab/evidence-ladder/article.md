## The takeaway

The AGD evidence ladder separates platform documentation, methodical proprietary data, controlled experiments, repeated observations, hypotheses and folklore. Every claim should retain provenance.

## The argument

Documentation may confirm a feature exists without proving its effect on reach. Internal observation may reveal a pattern without establishing a cause. Write the exact proposition supported by each source. Evidence level belongs to the claim, not author prestige or the number of links below it.

During review, underline verbs asserting a cause: increases, reduces, causes. Ask what evidence supports each one. A descriptive rewrite may be more accurate: a difference is observed and its cause remains unknown. This sentence-level work is more useful than a global “data-driven” badge above the article. Readers need to know which proposition a source actually supports, especially when one paragraph combines documented features with untested performance hypotheses.

## Make the claim fit the material underneath it

A platform document can establish that a feature exists. A public post can show how a creator used a format. An internal log can establish that an operation completed. A controlled experiment may support a narrower causal statement about an outcome. These are different kinds of evidence, not interchangeable decorations for the same headline. Before publishing a claim, ask what the cited material actually contains and whether a reader could follow the link and reach the same limited conclusion. Remove the part that depends on invisible assumptions.

Use explicit verbs. Documentation describes a capability; an observation shows what was visible at a stated time; a comparison suggests an association; a well-designed test can support an effect within its scope. Keep hypotheses interesting without laundering them into facts. For social examples, separate the observable hook from source-reported context and from commercial outcomes nobody has supplied. This discipline does not make an article timid. It lets the writer take a strong editorial position while giving the reader a clean boundary between judgement and evidence.

### Match the sentence to the evidence

- **Capability:** Official documentation describes what is supported.

- **Observation:** A dated record shows what was visible.

- **Causality:** A suitable design tests competing explanations.
