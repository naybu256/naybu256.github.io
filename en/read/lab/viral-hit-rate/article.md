## The takeaway

Viral Hit Rate is the proportion of eligible posts exceeding a predefined outlier threshold. Interpretation depends on threshold, cohort and window.

## The argument

In a fictional example, four hits among one hundred eligible posts yield 4%. If twenty more are too recent to evaluate, show them separately. Do not silently add them to the denominator. Retain uncertainty too: four events offer limited stability for predicting the next series.

Also display results by creative concept. Ten versions of one idea do not represent ten independent discoveries. Post-level rate answers a production question; concept-level rate helps describe creative discovery. Both can be useful, but should not merge into one unexplained score. Keeping their denominators separate prevents a heavily repeated successful idea from being mistaken for a pipeline that repeatedly discovers unrelated winners.

## Choose the denominator before celebrating

A hit rate is a fraction with politics hidden inside it. Suppose a team counts only published posts while quietly excluding abandoned edits, failed uploads and accounts it retired. That might be a reasonable publishing metric, but it is not the success rate of the entire operation. Define what qualifies as an attempt and what qualifies as a hit before the reporting period starts. Keep those definitions stable. If you move the threshold downward after a weak month, the chart becomes an argument for keeping the project alive rather than a useful description.

Report the number of attempts alongside the fraction. Two hits in a tiny batch and twenty in a much larger batch carry different uncertainty, even when the displayed percentage matches. Separate account-level concentration from post-level frequency: one unusually successful account can make a network look broadly healthy. Keep a second measure for meaningful downstream behaviour so entertainment reach does not silently become purchase intent. The point of this metric is to compare a defined process over time, not to turn a rare event into a promise.

### Three decisions inside one fraction

- **Attempt:** Which published, failed or abandoned units count?

- **Threshold:** What outcome qualifies, and at what age?

- **Scope:** Does the rate describe posts, accounts or campaigns?
