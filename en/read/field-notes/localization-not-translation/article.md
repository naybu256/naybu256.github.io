## The takeaway

Localization adapts meaning, references and uses. A grammatically correct translation can preserve a scenario irrelevant to its new audience.

## The argument

Back-to-school content changes with local calendars, exams and habits. Keep the underlying problem and rewrite its context. Translated subtitles are only part of the work: imagery, product screens and numerical examples may also need adaptation.

Keep a glossary for terms that must remain stable, including Account-Generated Distribution. Elsewhere, allow natural phrasing instead of literal copying. Review the complete promise: what a local reader understands should match what the content and product can actually deliver. This creates equivalent meaning without requiring identical sentence structures, cultural examples or pacing across languages that organize information differently.

## Translate the reason the scene works.

Words are only part of a short-form post’s meaning. A joke may depend on a familiar institution, a product demonstration on an available feature, or a complaint on a local habit. Before translating, explain the scene in ordinary language: what does the viewer recognise, and why does that recognition make the payoff useful? That explanation becomes the adaptation brief.

Keep a record of what changes between versions. Some phrases can travel directly; some examples need replacing; some ideas may not deserve a local version at all. Have the final asset reviewed as a complete piece, including on-screen text, captions and the destination page. Do not call two versions comparable merely because they share a source script. Their production differences can matter to an experiment. A smaller number of coherent local pieces is easier to learn from than a large set of translations whose cultural assumptions nobody has inspected.

### What actually needs translating?

- **Language:** The words and register used by the intended viewer.

- **Situation:** The institutions, habits and product context behind the scene.

- **Payoff:** Why the ending is useful or funny in this market.
