## The takeaway

Native Warming happens in the real application through ordinary, authentic interactions. The practice should remain meaningful to the person performing it.

## The argument

Providing interests and an observation goal is more intelligible than prescribing a choreography of likes. An operator can report relevant trends, language mismatches or off-topic content. Those observations have research value even when their effect on later distribution remains unknown.

A useful instruction might request three recurring question types in a niche. The number structures the report; it is not an algorithmic dose. Operators should remain free to report that no clear pattern emerged. Missing signal is information, not work to disguise so a form looks complete. This preserves the distinction between an authentic observation task and a mechanical requirement to perform a predetermined sequence.

## Replace vague activity with a documented purpose.

A preparation routine should have a reason someone can explain. Completing an account’s visible identity and learning the niche are different tasks from attempting to influence distribution through repeated actions. Put them in separate records. Otherwise a team can attribute a better creative brief to a technical routine simply because the two happened before the same successful post.

If the routine is being evaluated as an experiment, write down its exact scope, duration and completion criteria. Keep comparable creative work and observation windows across the accounts being compared, and record deviations. A small informal pilot may be useful for finding operational problems without being strong enough to support a causal claim. Say which kind of learning you obtained. Avoid turning a handful of anecdotes into instructions that everyone must perform the same sequence of actions. The account needs a coherent audience relationship; the evidence for any additional mechanism has to be earned separately.

### A routine with an inspectable claim

- **Define:** Describe what is done and why the team expects it to matter.

- **Compare:** Keep other meaningful differences visible.

- **Conclude:** Separate an operating observation from a causal result.

## TokPortal workflow: Turn an audience niche into explicit warming topics

An account about freelance money problems needs a more precise brief than “business content”. These are example topic fields for the documented Advanced Niche Warming option, to be combined with the rest of a validated bundle request.

Endpoint: POST /api/ext/bundles — body fragment

```json
{
  "wants_advanced_warming": true,
  "advanced_warming_terms": [
    "freelancer budget",
    "subscription tracking",
    "irregular income"
  ]
}
```

The topics describe a proposed editorial neighbourhood. They do not establish a causal reach advantage. A suitable brief keeps the operator’s actions relevant to the account instead of treating warming as a magic ceremony.

- **Use valid targets:** Supply 3–30 distinct terms, in multiples of three, with 2–50 characters per term.

- **Include the flag:** Targets without wants_advanced_warming: true can return ADVANCED_WARMING_FLAG_REQUIRED.

- **Inspect the result:** Use the recorded proof/data report for operational review. Do not present completion as proof of future reach.
