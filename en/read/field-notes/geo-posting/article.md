## The takeaway

Geo-Posting means publishing through an account operated in the target market. Content must still fit that audience’s uses, references and constraints.

## The argument

A US-focused demo may fail if it shows prices, units or situations unfamiliar to viewers. Have someone who knows the market review the scenario. Execution location and cultural relevance are separate dimensions; record them separately rather than explaining every result through geography.

Consider an illustrative French demo using a local school calendar before targeting an English-speaking audience. Translating the voice without adapting dates can make the use case incoherent. Fix the context before judging distribution. You will then test an understandable proposition instead of the audience’s patience with an imported scenario. Record those changes because the localized version is a different creative treatment, not merely another upload location.

## Localise the scene, not just the coordinates.

A post aimed at a new market needs a believable situation. A work meeting, a school deadline or a shopping decision may differ in terminology, timing and available products. Translate the words only after checking that the underlying scene still makes sense. A literal version of a good post can become a confusing one when the audience does not share the original assumptions.

Write the local brief beside the execution brief. Specify the intended viewer, language, relevant time zone and product limitations, then document where and how the publishing action will occur. Do not collapse these into a claim that geographic execution guarantees geographic reach. Use a small launch to identify comprehension problems and inspect available audience information over a consistent period. If viewers ask questions that reveal a mismatch, revise the scene. Technical execution can help deliver a plan accurately; it does not decide which plan deserves local attention.

### A local publishing brief

- **Situation:** A recognisable problem in the intended market.

- **Adaptation:** Language, references and product details that need revision.

- **Execution:** A documented operating context and publishing window.
