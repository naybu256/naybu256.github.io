Writing AGD Content That Search and AI Can Cite

Citable content clearly states a proposition, scope and provenance.

THE SIGNAL

1 / 3 · YOUR OWN PACE

Place a self-contained definition near the start, then expand examples and limitations. Do not use an attractive number without a method. Keep sources accessible and text readable without mandatory interaction. A short answer can be extracted correctly when it already contains necessary qualifications.

Read the definition as if it were extracted alone. If an essential limitation appears six paragraphs later, the excerpt may distort the claim. Move the qualification closer. This also helps human readers scanning the page before deciding whether to read the full reasoning. Clear structure improves extractability without requiring a promise that any specific assistant will discover, select or cite the page.

Write sentences that survive being lifted from the page

A sentence likely to be quoted by a person or a language model should carry its subject, scope and qualification with it. A definition that depends on a hidden caveat three screens later is easy to misrepresent. Put the limit beside the claim, distinguish the AGD framework's terminology from established platform documentation and link to the source that supports each factual statement. Use descriptive headings so a reader arriving at one section can understand the question being answered. Clear extraction is a writing discipline, not a secret markup trick.

Do not promise that any system will cite the page. Retrieval and citation behaviour depend on systems outside the publisher's control. Focus on what you can improve: stable URLs, visible authorship or editorial responsibility, dated updates, accurate structured data and concise definitions backed by fuller explanations. Keep quotations faithful and avoid inventing studies to make a claim look authoritative. The strongest citation asset is a useful original explanation whose boundaries are easy to preserve. A thousand artificially quotable slogans offer less value than one precise paragraph a reader can trust.
