## The takeaway

An editorial schema requires publishing essentials: identity, translations, media, stories, text, sources and links. It cannot guarantee an interesting article by itself.

## The argument

Separate mechanical validation from review: unique slugs and image provenance can be checked automatically. The soundness of an opinion and usefulness of an example need judgment. A mature pipeline also blocks broken references and missing translations instead of publishing a skeleton because its JSON is valid.

THE ORGANIC CLUB’s supplied schema requires two languages, an image with provenance, stories, body text and explicit limitations. Validation also checks references to other articles. These constraints make seeding reproducible. Review still owns subject distinctiveness and example relevance, neither of which structure alone certifies. Treat automated checks as necessary publishing conditions rather than a substitute for deciding whether the piece is actually worth reading.

## The schema is a floor for completeness

A strict article schema can prevent missing media, empty sources and broken relationships, but it cannot decide whether a paragraph is worth reading. Use the structure to make omissions visible: a clear point, developed reasoning, an explanatory visual, practical steps and limits. For a real social example, require a canonical post URL, creator attribution, a dated verification record and a distinction between observation and reported context. Reject arbitrary embed HTML. The publishing system should assemble known fields into a controlled component rather than trusting scraped markup.

Keep drafts separate from published records and make validation part of the build. A candidate discovered through a scraper is not an approved case study. Someone still needs to check relevance, rights, identity and what the evidence supports. Preserve a way to remove an unavailable embed without deleting the surrounding explanation. Review the corpus for repeated arguments and decorative visuals that add no understanding. Structure succeeds when it frees editorial attention for better examples and sharper reasoning, not when it makes every article sound as if the same form wrote it.

### From candidate to complete article

- **Draft:** An original argument with a specific reader question.

- **Verify:** Check sources, media, examples and limitations.

- **Publish:** Render validated fields with stable, accessible links.
