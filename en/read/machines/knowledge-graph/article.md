## The takeaway

An editorial graph connects parent concepts, neighbors and applications. Its usefulness comes from meaningful relationships, not automatically added link counts.

## The argument

Account Isolation naturally leads to Content Isolation: one differentiates accounts, the other what they publish. An MCP link fits when the reader moves into orchestration. Give anchors labels describing the next step. Crawlable links are still invitations addressed to a person first.

Avoid sending every article to the same three pages without reason. Relationships should follow the argument: a zero-view note leads to diagnosis and measurement quality, while a creative essay leads to personas and formats. The graph becomes navigable when every link announces a useful neighboring question. Automated suggestions can help, but should still be checked against what the source article actually discusses.

## Relationships should help someone choose the next question

A knowledge graph can begin as a disciplined set of links. Define each concept, assign a stable identifier and describe how it relates to others: depends on, contrasts with, measures or implements. Those relationships are more useful than attaching every vaguely related keyword to every article. A reader moving from account isolation to content isolation should understand why the distinction matters. Keep the vocabulary small enough to use consistently and let the editorial structure expose meaningful disagreements rather than flattening everything into one interchangeable network.

Validate links when concepts are renamed or retired. Avoid orphan pages and circular recommendations that send readers repeatedly through the same three articles without answering a new question. Use the graph to identify missing explanations, not merely to increase internal link counts. Structured data should reflect content actually present on the page and should use supported types accurately. The visible navigation remains the primary test: can a curious reader follow the relationship and learn something specific? If the graph is impressive only in an internal diagram, it has not yet earned its complexity.

### A useful relationship has a verb

- **Defines:** Explain the concept in its own article.

- **Connects:** Name why another concept matters here.

- **Guides:** Offer the next question a reader can resolve.

Editorial model: a way to reason about the process, not measured platform results.
