## The takeaway

SEO AGD explores social content and properties appearing in search engines and platform search. This possibility guarantees neither indexing nor ranking.

## The argument

Start with a question the content actually answers. Make context explicit in the title, caption or available transcript. Check that the public page is accessible. Content should remain useful in the feed: turning every sentence into keyword repetition can harm it without improving discovery.

A social tutorial can lead to a longer explanation preserving steps and limitations. The page then extends the subject instead of copying the caption. Check that the link makes sense to a viewer discovering the video without knowing the brand. Search extends useful information; it should not justify pointless duplication. Keep each surface valuable in its own context while making the connection understandable.

## The article must work without the feed

A swipeable home page can introduce a topic, but the article needs a stable address and a complete explanation of its own. Give it a descriptive title, a clear answer near the top, supporting sections and links that an ordinary browser can follow. Keep substantive text in the rendered HTML rather than hiding the argument inside an interaction. Search visitors may arrive halfway through the site's conceptual map. Define unfamiliar terms where they matter and provide routes to the deeper explanation without forcing a tour of the home feed.

Build the information architecture around real reader questions. A category page should help someone choose a useful next article, not merely repeat a list of keywords. Keep canonical URLs consistent, update links when paths change and avoid publishing near-duplicate pages for every wording variation. Measure whether readers can find and understand the content before treating indexing as a distribution guarantee. Healthy technical SEO makes a worthwhile article accessible and interpretable. It cannot create a reason to read a page whose only original contribution is its target query.

### A discoverable page is a complete page

- **Address:** A stable canonical URL with crawlable links.

- **Answer:** Useful text available in the initial HTML.

- **Context:** Sources, definitions and meaningful related reading.
